Privacy Policy for TikTok Video Scheduler

Last Updated: September 7, 2026

1. Overview

TikTok Video Scheduler ("we", "our", "the App") is a desktop application that allows users to schedule and publish their own videos to their own TikTok account using TikTok's official Content Posting API. We respect your privacy.

2. Data We Collect

a) TikTok OAuth Data: When you authorize our App, we receive an access token, refresh token, open_id, and basic profile information from TikTok via the video.publish scope. This is required to publish on your behalf.

b) Video Content: Video files (MP4) and captions you select in the App. These are stored locally on your device and only transmitted to TikTok servers via FILE_UPLOAD when you trigger a publish at your scheduled time.

c) No Third-Party Sharing: We do not sell, share, or transfer your TikTok data, access tokens, or videos to third parties.

3. How We Use Data

  • To authenticate you with TikTok OAuth and obtain a user access token.
  • To query creator_info/query to display your allowed privacy settings.
  • To initialize uploads via /v2/post/publish/video/init/ and upload video content.
  • To check publish status via /v2/post/publish/status/fetch/.

4. Data Storage and Security

Access tokens are stored locally on your device in an encrypted local database (SQLite) and are never transmitted to our servers because we operate a local desktop app with no backend. You can revoke access at any time from TikTok Settings > Authorized Apps.

5. Data Retention

We retain access tokens until you disconnect or revoke access. Videos remain on your local device. Published videos are subject to TikTok's own Privacy Policy.

6. Your Rights

You can: (a) Revoke authorization in TikTok, (b) Delete the local database (scheduler.db) to remove tokens, (c) Uninstall the App to delete all local data.

7. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for children under 13.
